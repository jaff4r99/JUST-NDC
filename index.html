Framework & Methodology

Instructional manual for the line-by-line coding process evaluated against four justice dimensions and four commitment tiers.

Recognitional Justice

Acknowledges marginalized groups, Indigenous rights, diverse values and knowledge systems, addressing exclusion.

Procedural Justice

Ensures transparent, inclusive and fair decision-making processes with meaningful participation.

Distributive Justice

Equitably shares benefits and burdens of climate actions and impacts across groups and generations.

Restorative Justice

Recognizes and seeks to address past climate injustices through reparative measures and responses.

Transformative Climate Justice

Addressing the root causes of climate change and social injustice.
